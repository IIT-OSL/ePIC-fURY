Read CLAUDE.md. Sprint 3: printable sheets for WSO2.
Make slides/wso2_sheets.pdf (A4, black on white) with:
1. The verification checklist (7 rows: check, how, pass when, result, initials): joint ordering, action scaling, observation scaling, control frequency, policy inputs, golden test, sim-to-sim.
2. The physical test protocol, stages 1 to 6, with the abort rule in a box at the top: oscillation, buzzing, joint at a limit, or sudden jump means switch to passive.
   Stages: (1) hung, passive, hand-move each joint; (2) hung, stand then policy with zero command 10 s; (3) on ground with harness, zero command 30 s; (4) forward 0.3 m/s for 3 m, 3 trials; (5) forward 0.5 m/s, turn 0.5 rad/s, sidestep 0.2 m/s, 3 trials each; (6) optional light side push.
3. A trial sheet table: trial #, stage, command, duration, success/fall, notes; 30 empty rows.
4. A bring list: deploy laptop, Ethernet cable + USB adapter, Unitree remote, harness or rope, phone tripod, charged battery, these sheets.
